---
title: Revival Sign "Carry On"
summary: whoever an error cut off mid-turn checks what landed, then carries on (cast it at failed)
---
Your last turn was cut off by an error - an API error, a dropped connection, a login that
lapsed - before you had finished. If it was not, and your last turn ended normally, say so in
one line and stop here.

Otherwise, carry on where you left off. First find out where things stand, because the step
you were on may be half-applied: a file partly edited, a command that may or may not have run,
a message that may already have gone out. Check rather than assume: `git status --short` and
`git diff` in {cwd}, the files that step touched, and the output or effect of whatever command
it had started.

Do not redo what is already done, and do not send again a message that already went. Finish
what is half-done, then go on with the rest of the task as it was. If you cannot tell what you
were in the middle of, or something you need is still failing, stop and tell me in a line or
two instead of guessing or retrying.
