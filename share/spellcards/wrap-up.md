We are finishing here. Please wrap up in this order:

1. Tell me in one short paragraph what you did this session and what is left undone.
2. Show me `git status --short` for {cwd} and name anything you changed that I have not
   seen, so nothing surprises me later.
3. Leave the tree in a state I can commit: no half-applied edits, no temporary files of
   yours, no debugging left switched on. Say what you removed.

Do not commit, do not push and do not start anything new. When you have said those three
things, stop and wait for me.
