---
title: Spirit Sign "Status Report"
summary: three lines from every resident: what you are on, where it stands, what is next
---
Stop and tell me where you are, in three short lines and nothing more:

1. what you are working on right now, in one sentence;
2. what state it is in - running, blocked, half-finished, waiting on me;
3. what you would do next if I said nothing.

Do not start anything new, do not run tests and do not edit any file to answer this. If you
were mid-task, say so in line 2 and carry on afterwards.
